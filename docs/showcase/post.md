# Rokela PDF: every PDF task, done beautifully

**Rokela PDF** is a PDF workspace built in Freetown, Sierra Leone. Edit, sign, merge and convert documents in the
browser or on an Android phone, with no watermarks and nothing to install. Most tools run entirely on your device,
so your files never leave it.

**What it does**

- **38 PDF tools:** merge, split, compress, rotate, crop, organize, watermark, page numbers, protect, unlock and redact
- **PDF editor:** add and replace text, highlight, draw, insert images, plus true redaction
- **Sign and stamp:** draw, type or upload a signature, add company stamps, fill and create forms, request signatures
- **Convert:** PDF to and from Word, Excel and PowerPoint, PDF to and from images, and OCR in five languages
- **Rokela Send:** send big files with passwords, expiry dates, download limits and download tracking
- **Short links and QR codes** with custom domains and click stats
- **Upload requests:** collect documents from anyone, with a checklist and a deadline
- **Verified documents:** issue PDFs with a QR code anyone can scan to confirm they are genuine
- **New, Receipt processing:** match students' bank receipts against the bank statement and issue fee clearance
  certificates
- **Receipt Maker:** receipts, invoices and quotations for small businesses, synced between web and phone

**Built with** Next.js, TypeScript, pdf-lib, pdf.js, Tesseract, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Cloudflare R2, Railway and
Capacitor for Android.
